##Proteomics_COVID-19_GCS
Analysis code for the manuscript "Immune-metabolic plasma proteomic signatures distinguish asymptomatic from symptomatic SARS-CoV-2 infection and predict post-COVID sequelae", based on plasma proteomic measurements in the Gutenberg COVID-19 Study (GCS).

##Contents

GCS_proteomics_analysis.R contains the analyses reported in the manuscript, including the protein score across infection severity groups, the associations with acute symptoms and post-COVID sequelae, the pre- and post-infection comparison, and the sensitivity analyses added during revision.

##Data

The underlying data cannot be made publicly available, as they contain individual participant information from a population-based cohort. Data access requests can be directed to the Gutenberg COVID-19 Study.

##Requirements

R 4.5.2 or later. The packages used are listed at the top of the script.
